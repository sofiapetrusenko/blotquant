Reading: the rule is conservative. It essentially never declares
agreement when true rho_s <= 0.70, rarely at 0.80, and declares it
only ~40% of the time even when agreement (0.90) is exactly true,
because small-N Spearman is biased low. The error direction is the
safe one for this project: false modesty, not false confidence.
